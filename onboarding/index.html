How onboarding works

Three things happen in parallel. None of them can be skipped, and you are not on site with students until the last one is confirmed.

1 · Your site's clearanceEach host site runs its own background check. Pick your site below and follow that path. A clearance from one site does not transfer to another.
2 · SOTC's own requirementsOne free online training before you start and one within 30 days, a signed packet, your agreement and tax form, all sent through the upload form. Same for every site.
3 · Upload, then wait for the wordEverything goes through one secure upload form. SOTC tells you the day the site confirms clearance. That is your start date.

Step 1 · Your site's clearance path

Your assignment email names your site. Choose it here.

East Oakland Pride Elementary · Oakland Unified · Safe Passages after-school

OUSD clears vendor staff through its Be A Mentor "Contractors Online" system, and Safe Passages, the after-school operator, keeps the roster of cleared instructors. Both have to say yes.

  1. SOTC submits your name to OUSD. You then receive an email from help@beamentor.org with the OUSD Request for Live Scan form attached. If it has not arrived within a few days, email info@scienceonthecourt.com; the form is not posted publicly.
  2. Get fingerprinted with that form. OUSD's partner is A1 Fingerprinting at Howard Terminal, 1 Market St, Oakland. Call (510) 836-0448 for an appointment, bring the form, your photo ID and payment, and say you are a "Be a Mentor vendor" for the discount. Any other Live Scan operator also works, for example Wellnessmart in Oakland or Tarrell Livescan in Hayward.
  3. Keep the copy the operator hands back. The ATI number printed on it is what Safe Passages needs. Photograph it before you leave the counter and submit it through the upload form.
  4. TB test. A negative result dated within the last three years, from any clinic, pharmacy or student health center. Send the result, not the receipt.
  5. Mandated Reporter training: the General module and the School Personnel module at mandatedreporterca.com. Free. Safe Passages requires the School Personnel certificate specifically.
  6. Proof of education: a photo of your diploma, or an unofficial transcript showing 48 or more credits.
  7. Photo ID, unexpired, and a current resume, submitted through the upload form.
  8. SOTC adds you to the Safe Passages certification roster and sends it to their HR. You are cleared when Safe Passages confirms in writing. Not before.

Cost: roughly $100 for Live Scan and TB combined, reimbursed on your first invoice after clearance. Keep every receipt.

John Muir Middle School · San Leandro Unified · Boys & Girls Clubs of San Leandro

San Leandro USD clears you through its own Live Scan, and its Expanded Learning office confirms when you are clear. An OUSD clearance does not count here, and the reverse is also true, because each district's form carries its own agency code.

  1. SOTC emails you the SLUSD Request for Live Scan form. It is specific to the district and is not posted publicly.
  2. Get fingerprinted with that form at any Live Scan operator. Tarrell Livescan, 27526 Tampa Ave, Hayward, has been the fastest for our staff. Bring the form, your photo ID and payment. Keep the copy the operator hands back, photograph the ATI number, and submit it through the upload form.
  3. TB clearance. A negative result within the last three years. The district asks for it as its own file, so send it as a clear scan or PDF, not a phone photo of a photo.
  4. Mandated Reporter training: General and School Personnel modules at mandatedreporterca.com. Free.
  5. Photo ID, proof of education, and a current resume, all through the upload form.
  6. SOTC lists you on the district's Required Certifications form and forwards your TB clearance. The district's HR processes the Live Scan result; delays of several weeks happen with no explanation. You are cleared when the Expanded Learning office confirms.

Cost: Live Scan and TB, reimbursed on your first invoice after clearance.

Allen Temple Baptist Church · East Oakland

The church vets program staff the way it vets its own volunteers: a name-based background check through Sterling Volunteers (formerly Verified Volunteers) plus the church's volunteer orientation session. No fingerprinting is required for this site alone.

  1. Sterling Volunteers check. SOTC orders it. You receive an email from Sterling Volunteers with a link to enter your identifying information and consent. It runs a national criminal database search, county records and the sex-offender registry, usually back within a few days.
  2. Church volunteer session. The church's campus manager schedules a general orientation for volunteers; SOTC tells you the date. Attendance is required before you work with students there.
  3. Mandated Reporter training, General module at mandatedreporterca.com, and SafeSport within 30 days of starting (see Step 3).
  4. Photo ID, proof of education, and a current resume, all through the upload form.
  5. SOTC sends the church an affidavit listing each cleared staff member, the provider and the check date. You are cleared when that affidavit includes you and the church has confirmed the session.

If you will also work at a district site, you still need that district's Live Scan. The church check does not replace it.

Camps, conferences, one-day events and new sites

Every site has a host, and the host decides the screening. Before any event, SOTC tells you which of the three paths above applies, or whether the host accepts SOTC's own screening. Whatever the host says, SOTC's own requirements in Step 2 apply everywhere, and the two-adult rule applies from the first minute.

Step 2 · Required of every instructor, every site

These come from the SOTC Operations Policy Manual and the insurance coverage that lets us work in schools. Download the manual below; you sign that you have read it.

  1. Independent Contractor Agreement, sent to you individually for electronic signature. Fill in your address on page 1, sign page 3.
  2. SOTC personnel packet, pages 1 to 3: application with five-year history and references, background-check notice, and the acknowledgments page. Fillable PDF. Download.
  3. Code of Conduct, one page, signed. Fillable PDF. Download.
  4. Operations Policy Manual, read before you sign the packet. Download.
  5. IRS Form W-9, completed and signed. Blank form at irs.gov. Give it to SOTC through the upload form; never email it.
  6. Two professional references with name, relationship and contact. Not family.
  7. Headshot for the school newsletter and your site introduction.
  8. The two trainings in Step 3, with certificates: Mandated Reporter before you start, SafeSport within 30 days.

Signing electronically

Nothing here needs a printer. Every SOTC form is a fillable PDF: open it in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free, phone or computer), Apple Preview, or your phone's Files app, type into the boxes, and sign one of two ways.

  • Type your full legal name in the signature box. Submitted through the upload form, which records your email and the time, that is a valid electronic signature under the federal ESIGN Act and California's UETA.
  • Or draw it: Acrobat Reader's Fill & Sign, Preview's Markup, or iPhone Markup all place a drawn signature on any PDF.

The W-9 from the IRS is already fillable and the IRS accepts an electronic signature on it. The Independent Contractor Agreement arrives by email through an e-signature service, so you sign it from the link with no download at all.

Step 4 · Send everything through the secure form

One Google Form with file-upload questions. Your files land in a private folder that only SOTC's Executive Director can open. They are never emailed and never shared with schools.

Open the SOTC Instructor Clearance Upload form

Google requires a sign-in to any Google account for file uploads, including a free Gmail address; that is what lets your files go straight into a private folder instead of over email. You do not need a Science on the Court account. Phone photos are fine when every word is readable. If something is still in progress, submit what you have now and add the rest later; the form lets you edit your response.

Never email your ID, W-9, TB result or Live Scan form. If you would rather hand over originals, bring them to your first afternoon and they are scanned on the spot.

Pay, invoices and reimbursement

  • Rate: per your Independent Contractor Agreement, for in-person instruction.
  • Invoices are accepted on the 1st and 15th of each month, for the period just ended, emailed to info@scienceonthecourt.com. Miss the date and payment waits for the next one.
  • Each invoice shows: invoice number, service period, one line per session with date, site, hours and rate, the total, your contact details, and your Zelle or direct-deposit choice.
  • Reimbursements for Live Scan, TB and SafeSport go on your first invoice after clearance, with receipts attached. Anything else needs SOTC's approval in advance.
  • Payment follows within 7 business days by Zelle or direct deposit.

Questions

If you have a question, or you are not sure how to proceed, email info@scienceonthecourt.com. Your site assignment email gives you a direct contact as well.

If you are stuck on a step for more than a few days, say so. Clearance delays on the district side are common and are not your fault, but we can only chase what we know about.
