ParentStream Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

ParentStream is a text-message service run by Science on the Court ("we", "us") for parents and guardians of students in our programs. This policy explains what we collect when you sign up, how we use it, and your choices.

What we collect

  • From you at sign-up: your name, your mobile number, and your child's first name and school.
  • Your opt-in record: the START text you send from your phone, with its date and time.
  • About your child's school work: assignment, due-date, submission, and grade information from your school's parent or student portal, which you authorize us to read on your behalf. We only retrieve information about the children you enroll.
  • Your messages to us: questions or replies you text to the ParentStream number.

How we use it

  • To send you at most two text updates a day about your own child's school work, plus an occasional short parenting tip.
  • To answer questions you text us about your child's work.
  • To keep a record of your consent and your opt-out, as the law requires.

We do not use this information for advertising, and we never send marketing texts.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with anyone for any purpose other than delivering this service.

We use service providers only to operate ParentStream: a telecommunications provider to send and receive the texts, and an automated writing service to turn your child's school data into a short, readable message. These providers process the information on our behalf and under our instructions, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

We will disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of a child.

Children

ParentStream texts parents and guardians, not children. Information about a child is provided by, and used only for, that child's parent or guardian. If you believe a child has signed up directly, text or email us and we will remove the entry.

Your choices

  • Stop: reply STOP to any message and the texts end immediately.
  • Help: reply HELP for contact information.
  • Delete: email info@scienceonthecourt.com and we will delete your sign-up information and your child's retrieved school data.

Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

Retention and security

We keep your sign-up and consent records for as long as you are enrolled and for the period the law requires afterward. Retrieved school data is kept only as long as needed to send you current updates. Data is stored on access-controlled systems and is not published.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters to you, we will text you a notice before the change takes effect.
