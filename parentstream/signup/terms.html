ParentStream Terms of Service

Effective October 26, 2026

ParentStream is a text-message (SMS) service operated by Science on the Court ("we", "us") for parents and guardians of students in our programs. By signing up on our website and texting START to (833) 906-8053, you agree to these terms.

1. The program

ParentStream sends you short text messages about your own child's school work: assignments, due dates, missing work, grades, and upcoming tests, each with one concrete thing you can ask or do. Once a week it may also send a short parenting tip. You can text a question back at any time and we will answer it.

2. Message frequency

Up to 2 messages per day, sent in a morning window before school and an early-evening window. On days with nothing worth acting on, we send nothing. Replies to questions you text us are in addition to this.

3. Cost

ParentStream is free during the pilot. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier's plan. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

4. How to opt in

You enroll by filling in the sign-up form at our sign-up page, checking the consent box, and then texting START to (833) 906-8053 from the mobile number you entered. Your START text is your opt-in record. We will reply with a confirmation message.

5. How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive one final confirmation text and no further messages. To rejoin, text START again.

6. Help and support

Reply HELP to any message for contact information, or email info@scienceonthecourt.com, or call (510) 213-8563.

7. Carriers

Supported carriers include AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, and most other major U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission from your network operator.

8. Your responsibilities

  • Enroll only a mobile number you own and are authorized to use.
  • Enroll only children for whom you are the parent or legal guardian.
  • Tell us if you change your mobile number, by emailing us or texting STOP from the old number and signing up again.

9. Privacy

How we collect, use, and protect your information is described in our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

10. No guarantees

ParentStream summarizes information from your child's school portal and may be incomplete, delayed, or contain errors. It is not a substitute for the school's own records, and we are not responsible for decisions made on the basis of a text. The service is provided as is, and we may change, pause, or end it at any time.

11. Changes to these terms

If these terms change in a way that matters to you, we will text you a notice before the change takes effect. Continuing to receive messages after that means you accept the updated terms.
