Rules & Tiebreakers

  • Pool match: single 30-sec round.
  • Final: best of 3 rounds.
  • 3-2-1-SUMO — both press Play.
  • Hands off after start.
  • Tweaks OK between rounds, no rebuilds.
  • Win a round: push opponent fully out, or be closest to center at time.
  • Stuck 10 sec: other bot wins.
  • Pool winner: best record.
  • Tie → speed of victory: push-out beats a decision; faster push-out ranks higher.
