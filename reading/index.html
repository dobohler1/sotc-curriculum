How every final works

  • Part 1 · Reading check. Closed book. One question from every chapter. Your first try is the score. Miss too many and you reread those chapters and answer a new question from each, book open, with the page number.
  • Part 2 · ISEE reading skills. Passages with numbered lines. Every answer needs the line number that proves it. The target is 90% on the first try; below that, you earn back each missing point one question at a time.
  • Stay on the test. Switching to another window, tab, or app is recorded.
